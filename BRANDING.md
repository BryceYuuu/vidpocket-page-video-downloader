# Branding

## Name

- English: **VidPocket**
- Chinese: **视频口袋**

## Positioning

VidPocket is a local-first Chrome webpage video downloader for directly accessible media resources and unencrypted HLS streams.

It is not positioned as a protected-stream extractor, DRM bypass tool, or platform-specific downloader.
